EVENT RULES & TERMS

By creating and operating an event on this Event System, the Event Creator agrees to comply with all Event Rules & Terms.

1. EVENT CREATOR RESPONSIBILITY
The Event Creator is responsible for the accuracy of all event information, lot descriptions, photographs, prices, banking details and other information supplied for the event.

2. PROHIBITED ITEMS AND CONTENT
No human beings or sale of people, pornography or sexual/adult content, animal cruelty, firearms, ammunition, explosives, illegal goods or other prohibited items may be advertised, offered or sold through the Event System.

3. BUYER PAYMENTS
Successful bidders will automatically receive an invoice for their winning purchases. Payment is made directly to the Event Creator's registered bank account. The buyer and Event Creator are responsible for confirming payment between them.

4. EVENT SYSTEM
The Event System provides the online platform for creating, advertising and conducting the event and does not take possession of purchase money paid by buyers directly to Event Creators.

5. BINDING BIDS
All bids placed during a LIVE event are binding. The highest successful bidder is responsible for payment of the winning bid and any applicable charges stated in the event terms.

6. EVENT CREATOR'S TERMS
The Event Creator must clearly state any additional conditions relating to collection, delivery, shipping, export, buyer's costs or other requirements applicable to the event or individual lots.

7. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION
The Event Creator must confirm payment only after verifying that the payment has actually been received. A payment notification, screenshot or proof of payment alone should not be treated as confirmation that cleared funds have been received.

8. NON-PAYMENT
Non-payment of a winning invoice may result in the bidder's access to the Event System being restricted or suspended until the outstanding matter has been resolved.

9. LOT INFORMATION
The Event Creator must describe each lot as accurately as reasonably possible. Any known material defects, restrictions or important conditions should be disclosed to bidders before bidding closes.

10. PHOTOGRAPHS AND CONTENT
The Event Creator must have the right or permission to use all photographs, logos, descriptions and other content uploaded to the Event System.

11. COLLECTION, DELIVERY AND SHIPPING
Unless otherwise stated in the event terms, arrangements and costs for collection, delivery, shipping or export are between the Event Creator and the successful bidder.

12. DISPUTES BETWEEN BUYER AND EVENT CREATOR
Any dispute concerning the condition of a lot, delivery, collection, payment or fulfilment of a sale must first be addressed directly between the successful bidder and the Event Creator.

13. TEST MODE
An event may be created and tested before going LIVE. Bids, invoices and transactions made while the event is in test mode are for testing purposes only and do not constitute real purchases or sales.

14. GOING LIVE
The Event Creator is responsible for checking the event, lot information, banking details and settings before choosing to go LIVE. Once LIVE, genuine bids may be placed by registered bidders.

15. ONE-TIME ACTIVATION FEE
Creating and testing an event is free of charge. The applicable one-time activation fee becomes payable when an Event Creator chooses to activate their first LIVE event. Returning Event Creators who have already paid the applicable activation fee are not charged the activation fee again.

16. ACCEPTANCE OF RULES
By ticking “I ACCEPT THE EVENT RULES & TERMS”, the Event Creator confirms that they have read, understood and accepted these Rules & Terms before creating or operating a LIVE event.

Clivia Dreamers Auction — Rules & Terms

By registering as a buyer or seller, you agree to these rules.

Buyer Rules

1. Buyers must register with correct contact details and a full delivery address.

2. A valid bank account number must be supplied and confirmed before normal bidding is allowed.

3. Bids must comply with the bidding increment displayed for the applicable auction or lot. ZAR and EUR auctions may have different bidding increments.

4. A bidder receives the initial bidding capacity applicable to the auction. Additional bidding capacity is unlocked by paying the applicable security fee. For standard ZAR brackets, R150 unlocks the next R5,000 bidding capacity. For standard EUR brackets, €15 unlocks the next €350 bidding capacity.

5. Security fees increase bidding capacity only after the required payment has been confirmed. If proof of payment is awaiting verification, the additional bidding capacity remains locked until verification is completed.

6. A winning bidder must pay the seller directly for the winning plant within the required payment period. Any separate Clivia Dreamers platform fees or other amounts payable to Clivia Dreamers must be paid according to the relevant invoice.

7. A bidder with an unpaid invoice may be blocked from further bidding until the outstanding invoice has been paid.

8. A payment received from an unregistered or unconfirmed bank account may place the bidder's account on hold until the payment has been verified or the hold has been cleared by Admin.

9. International bidders must pay the applicable international registration fee before bidding and may bid only on plants specifically marked as export eligible.

10. International buyers are responsible for applicable export, import, phytosanitary, courier and destination-country requirements and costs unless expressly stated otherwise.

11. Bids are binding. A bidder must not place a bid unless willing and able to complete the purchase if that bid wins.

12. Where an auction uses an extended-closing or anti-sniping period, a qualifying late bid may extend the closing time of that particular lot in accordance with the auction settings.

13. OWNER TEST MODE is strictly an administrative testing facility. Test-mode exemptions do not apply to ordinary registered bidders and do not create any entitlement to free bidding, waived fees or waived payment obligations.

Seller Rules

14. Sellers must provide accurate plant descriptions, photographs and seller details. Breeding names and a small copyright mark are allowed on plant photos.

15. Flowers containing more than 20% orange colour may not qualify for the auction. The AI system may check the flower photo automatically. If the AI is uncertain, the plant will be referred for Admin review rather than automatically rejected.

16. Sellers are responsible for ensuring that the plant offered for sale corresponds with the photographs and description supplied for the lot.

17. Where a seller is VAT registered and has supplied a VAT number, the Clivia Dreamers platform fee is calculated on the sale amount excluding the seller's VAT portion. No Clivia Dreamers platform fee is charged on the VAT portion of that sale.

18. Where applicable, the Clivia Dreamers platform fee is 10% of the applicable sale amount less the R50 listing fee, and applies only where the sale exceeds R2,000, subject to the auction rules and settings.

19. Clivia Dreamers is not VAT registered and therefore does not add VAT to its own platform-fee invoice.

Export Services

20. Export service provider details are not displayed publicly.

21. An R500 export connection fee applies before export-service contact details are released.

Payments & Privacy

18. Payment information may be checked against registered bank account details and payment amounts.

19. Buyer delivery/contact details are shared only where required to complete a winning order.

20. Payment for a winning item is made directly by the buyer to the seller. Clivia Dreamers Auction is not the recipient of the purchase payment.

21. Any transaction, payment, delivery or other arrangement between a buyer and seller is undertaken at their own risk. Clivia Dreamers Auction provides the auction platform but is not a party to the sale between the buyer and seller.

26. All Clivia Dreamers platform fees, listing fees, registration/security fees and export connection fees are non-refundable once paid, except where otherwise required by law.

23. Clivia Dreamers Auction may update these rules when necessary. Updated rules may require acceptance again.

24. The bidder and seller/owner are responsible for completing the transaction, payment and delivery arrangements between themselves.

25. Clivia Dreamers Auction does not guarantee that any plant listed for auction will receive bids or be sold. Sellers are encouraged to actively promote and share their auction plants through Facebook, WhatsApp, social media and any other sharing or advertising options provided by Clivia Dreamers Auction. These promotional tools are made available to all sellers to help increase exposure, but the responsibility for promoting a listed plant and attracting potential bidders remains with the seller.

26.Clivia Dreamers shall not be liable for delays, interruptions, postponement, cancellation or failure to perform caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, fire, flood, severe weather, war, civil unrest, government action, widespread power or telecommunications failures, strikes, epidemics or pandemics. Where reasonably possible, Clivia Dreamers may postpone, extend, suspend or reschedule an affected auction or event.

Gentleman's Agreement

Clivia Dreamers Auction operates in the spirit of trust, fairness and respect between buyers and sellers. Please honour your bids, payments, sales and commitments, and do not abuse the opportunity or privileges provided by this platform.
