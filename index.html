EVENT RULES & TERMS

By creating and operating an event on this Event System, the Event Creator agrees to comply with all Event Rules & Terms.

1. EVENT CREATOR RESPONSIBILITY
The Event Creator is responsible for the accuracy of all event information, lot descriptions, photographs, prices, banking details and other information supplied for the event.

2. PROHIBITED ITEMS AND CONTENT
No human beings or sale of people, pornography or sexual/adult content, animal cruelty, firearms, ammunition, explosives, illegal goods or other prohibited items may be advertised, offered or sold through the Event System.

3. BUYER PAYMENTS
Successful bidders will automatically receive an invoice for their winning purchases. Payment is made directly to the Event Creator's registered bank account. The buyer and Event Creator are responsible for confirming payment between them.

4. EVENT SYSTEM
The Event System provides the online platform for creating, advertising and conducting the event and does not take possession of purchase money paid by buyers directly to Event Creators.

5. BINDING BIDS
All bids placed during a LIVE event are binding. The highest successful bidder is responsible for payment of the winning bid and any applicable charges stated in the event terms.

6. EVENT CREATOR'S TERMS
The Event Creator must clearly state any additional conditions relating to collection, delivery, shipping, export, buyer's costs or other requirements applicable to the event or individual lots.

7. PAYMENT CONFIRMATION
The Event Creator must confirm payment only after verifying that the payment has actually been received. A payment notification, screenshot or proof of payment alone should not be treated as confirmation that cleared funds have been received.

8. NON-PAYMENT
Non-payment of a winning invoice may result in the bidder's access to the Event System being restricted or suspended until the outstanding matter has been resolved.

9. LOT INFORMATION
The Event Creator must describe each lot as accurately as reasonably possible. Any known material defects, restrictions or important conditions should be disclosed to bidders before bidding closes.

10. PHOTOGRAPHS AND CONTENT
The Event Creator must have the right or permission to use all photographs, logos, descriptions and other content uploaded to the Event System.

11. COLLECTION, DELIVERY AND SHIPPING
Unless otherwise stated in the event terms, arrangements and costs for collection, delivery, shipping or export are between the Event Creator and the successful bidder.

12. DISPUTES BETWEEN BUYER AND EVENT CREATOR
Any dispute concerning the condition of a lot, delivery, collection, payment or fulfilment of a sale must first be addressed directly between the successful bidder and the Event Creator.

13. TEST MODE
An event may be created and tested before going LIVE. Bids, invoices and transactions made while the event is in test mode are for testing purposes only and do not constitute real purchases or sales.

14. GOING LIVE
The Event Creator is responsible for checking the event, lot information, banking details and settings before choosing to go LIVE. Once LIVE, genuine bids may be placed by registered bidders.

15. ONE-TIME ACTIVATION FEE
Creating and testing an event is free of charge. The applicable one-time activation fee becomes payable when an Event Creator chooses to activate their first LIVE event. Returning Event Creators who have already paid the applicable activation fee are not charged the activation fee again.

16. ACCEPTANCE OF RULES
By ticking “I ACCEPT THE EVENT RULES & TERMS”, the Event Creator confirms that they have read, understood and accepted these Rules & Terms before creating or operating a LIVE event.

Buyer Rules

1. Buyers must register using accurate contact information and a valid delivery or collection address.

2. Bidders must complete any identity, account or bank verification required by the platform before bidding.

3. Every bid must meet the starting price and minimum bid increment displayed for its auction and currency.

5. Winning bids are binding, subject to applicable law and the published event terms. Bid only when able to pay.

6. Winning bidders receive an invoice after the auction closes and must pay the seller or Event Creator directly using the verified payment instructions on the invoice.

7. Unpaid winning invoices may lead to bidding restrictions while the outstanding payment is resolved.

8. Where enabled, qualifying last-minute bids may extend the closing time of the affected lot under the auction’s hot-bidding rules.

9. International buyers are responsible for any applicable import, export, customs, transport and destination-country requirements, unless the event terms state otherwise.

Seller and Event Creator Rules

10. Sellers and Event Creators must supply accurate descriptions, photographs, starting bids, increments and material condition information for each lot.

11. Only goods and content permitted by the platform and applicable law may be listed. Prohibited listings include people, sexual or pornographic material, cruelty, firearms, ammunition, explosives and illegal goods.

12. Sellers must have the right to offer the goods and to use uploaded photographs, logos and descriptions.

13. Event Creators must state collection, shipping, delivery, export, tax and other relevant terms before bidding closes.

14. Creating and testing an event is free. The applicable one-time activation fee is payable before the first LIVE event; returning Event Creators who have already paid it are not charged again.

15. The Event Creator is responsible for checking auction dates, closing times, banking details and lot information before making an event LIVE.

16. Test-mode bids and invoices are demonstrations only, not genuine sales or payment obligations.

Payments, Privacy and Responsibility

17. Buyer payments for winning lots go directly to the seller or Event Creator. What a Auction Event does not receive those purchase payments.

18. The seller or Event Creator must verify that cleared funds have been received before confirming payment. A screenshot or payment notification alone is not confirmation.

19. Payment and bank information may be verified for account security and reconciliation. Private contact and delivery details are shared only when needed to complete a winning transaction.

20. Buyers and sellers are responsible for completing payment, collection and delivery arrangements and resolving disputes about the goods or fulfilment, subject to applicable consumer law.

21. What a Auction Event provides the auction platform and does not guarantee that a listed lot will receive a bid or sell. Sellers are encouraged to share their listings using the available promotional tools.

22. Platform fees, where applicable, are described in the relevant published fee schedule or invoice. Refunds and cancellations are governed by those terms and applicable law.

23. What a Auction Event may revise its rules and request renewed acceptance when appropriate.

24. Events may be postponed or interrupted by circumstances beyond reasonable control, including severe weather, outages, civil unrest or government action. The platform will communicate material changes where reasonably possible.

Gentleman’s Agreement

UNSOLD PLANTS — AUTOMATIC CARRY-FORWARD

Unsold plants will automatically be entered into the next three scheduled Clivia Dreamers auction days.

The original starting price will remain unchanged unless the seller requests a price change.

Sellers will be notified when their unsold plants are automatically carried forward. No additional confirmation is required.

Unsold plants will be allocated additional spaces. There is no fixed limit on carry-forward lots.

After three additional auction appearances, any plant that remains unsold will no longer be listed.

WHAT A AUCTION EVENT — BANK VERIFICATION RULES

1. Every bidder must provide a valid bank statement or official bank confirmation clearly showing the account holder's name and bank account number.

2. The bank account number must match the number registered on the bidder's "What a Auction Event" profile.

3. Verified bank account details will be retained securely for relevant invoice and payment reconciliation.

4. Any later discrepancy between registered banking details and verified payment information may result in a warning and temporary suspension of bidding privileges until the discrepancy is resolved.

HOW RETAIL VOUCHERS, MONEY TRANSFERS AND ONLINE PAYMENTS WORK

"What a Auction Event" offers winning bidders convenient online and retail voucher payment options, subject to the seller’s acceptance and approval. All payments are made directly to the seller, and the seller must confirm receipt of payment.

  1. Winning Bidder: Receives an invoice for the winning auction lot and requests available payment options from the seller.
  2. Seller: Confirms which payment methods are accepted: retail vouchers, money transfers or bank payments.

IMPORTANT: All payment arrangements are between the winning bidder and the seller. Payment services are subject to availability and seller acceptance.

"What a Auction Event" does not receive or hold payments between buyers and sellers.
